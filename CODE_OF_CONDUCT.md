# Community guidelines

This project is intended to be a respectful and safe place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Respect participants regardless of background or experience.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Provide verifiable information and reproduction steps.
- Protect privacy: do not publish other people's files, metadata, or identifying
  information.
- Accept corrections and keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, abusive comments, disclosure of private information,
spam, and deliberate disruption of the project are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Content that violates these guidelines may be removed and an author's access
may be limited. Report serious concerns privately through GitHub's security or
abuse reporting features. If private reporting is unavailable, contact
[git@vms.net.pl](mailto:git@vms.net.pl).
